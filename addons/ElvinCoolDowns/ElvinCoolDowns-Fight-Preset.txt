==============================================================
 ElvinCoolDowns  -  Raid Fight Tracking Preset
==============================================================

HOW TO IMPORT
  1. In game, type:  /ecd io
  2. (Optional backup) click "Export" first and copy that text
     somewhere safe - Import will overwrite your tracked list.
  3. Select the whole ElvinCoolDowns:v1:... line below, copy it,
     paste it into the box, and click "Import".
  4. The tracked spells update immediately and their bars show.

Import REPLACES your custom tracked-spell settings only. It does
not touch your other options (layout, colours, message, etc.).

Every spell in this preset is set to both TRACK (log/monitor) and
SHOW (display a bar). In this addon those are separate: a spell can
be tracked without being shown. If you want any of these logged but
hidden, un-tick "Show" for it in the options Spells tab afterwards.

--------------------------------------------------------------
 WHAT THIS TRACKS
--------------------------------------------------------------
Raid / encounter cooldowns people call for during fights:

  Priest    Divine Hymn, Hymn of Hope, Guardian Spirit,
            Pain Suppression, Power Infusion, Fear Ward
  Paladin   Hand of Protection / Sacrifice / Salvation / Freedom,
            Divine Sacrifice, Aura Mastery, Lay on Hands, Divine Shield
  Druid     Rebirth, Innervate, Tranquility, Barkskin
  Shaman    Heroism / Bloodlust, Mana Tide Totem, Reincarnation
  Warlock   Soulstone Resurrection
  Hunter    Misdirection
  Rogue     Tricks of the Trade
  Warrior   Vigilance, Shield Wall, Retaliation
  DK        Anti-Magic Zone, Hysteria, Vampiric Blood
  Mage      Focus Magic

Specials (raid utility / "fish etc"):
  Fish Feast, Gigantic Feast, Ritual of Refreshment (mage table),
  Ritual of Souls (soulwell)

The feast/table entries have no real cooldown - they show a bar per
player who can/does drop one, so you can see who has a feast up.

--------------------------------------------------------------
 TIPS
--------------------------------------------------------------
- Want ONLY these and none of the addon's built-in defaults?
  Turn off the default spell list in the options (Spells tab) so the
  tracked set is exactly this preset.
- To share your own setup: /ecd io -> Export -> copy the string.
- To tweak: import this, then use the Spells tab to add/remove
  spells or toggle Show/Track per spell; changes save to your setup.
